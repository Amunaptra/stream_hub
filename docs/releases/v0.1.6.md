# Stream Hub v0.1.6

Release status: **automated validation passed; physical multicast acceptance pending**

## UDP/RTP multicast support

- Hub and Odroid playlist models accept MPEG-TS/UDP and RTP multicast URLs in
  `udp://239.x.x.x:port` and `rtp://239.x.x.x:port` form.
- VLC-style `udp://@239.x.x.x:port` and `rtp://@239.x.x.x:port` inputs are
  accepted and normalized to the MPV/FFmpeg-compatible canonical form.
- UDP/RTP destinations are restricted to IPv4 multicast space (`224.0.0.0/4`)
  and require an explicit port. Unicast targets, credentials, paths, query
  strings and fragments are rejected.
- Player preflight and periodic device-side health checks use bounded FFprobe
  analysis to confirm that packets contain decodable media tracks.
- The persistent MPV process and its existing `loadfile replace` transition,
  playback watchdog and reconnect behavior remain unchanged.
- Device capabilities now advertise `udp-rtp-multicast`.
